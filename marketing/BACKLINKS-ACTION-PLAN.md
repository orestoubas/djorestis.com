# Backlinks & listings — the working checklist

**Short answer to "I need backlinks, right?" — yes, but with an important correction.**

Backlinks are the binding constraint on your organic rankings: your competitor has ~15
years of domain age and accumulated links, and no amount of on-page work substitutes for
that. But for a *local service business*, raw backlink volume is not the lever people
assume. Three things matter more, in this order:

1. **Google Business Profile + reviews** — ~32% of local ranking signal, and the map pack
   takes 44–58% of clicks. This outranks every backlink you could earn this quarter.
2. **Prominence on the right industry sites** — being listed on eventplanner.be or House of
   Weddings is worth more than fifty generic directory links. In 2026, three of the top five
   factors for *AI-search* visibility are citation factors: presence on expert-curated
   "best of" lists, prominence on top industry-relevant domains, and quality of unstructured
   mentions (news, blogs, associations).
3. **Then** general citations — which now influence only ~6–7% of local results and exist
   mainly to confirm your name, address and phone are consistent.

**Do not buy bulk citation packages.** Services selling "200 directory submissions" are
nullified by Google's SpamBrain at best and harmful at worst. Mine their lists for names;
never pay them.

**Consistency rule for every listing below:** identical name, address and phone everywhere.
Use `DJ Orestis` · Brussels, Belgium · info@djorestis.com · https://djorestis.com. Mismatches
actively hurt.

## Must avoid

- **Bulk citation services** — Loganix, CitationBuilderPro, CitationForge, SERPninja and
  "200 best citation sites" packages. Nullified or harmful.
- **`http://bedrijftoevoegen-goudengids.be/`** — a hyphenated plain-HTTP **lookalike** of the
  real Goudengids signup subdomain. Do not submit your data there.
- **Bark's "Belgium" DJ page** — it is `bark.com/.../wisconsin/belgium/`, meaning Belgium,
  **Wisconsin**. The same trap exists for "Amsterdam, New York".
- **TripAdvisor** (you have no premises), **Wikipedia / self-made Wikidata** (notability fail).
- Link farms: biz.prlog.org/be, be.bizin.eu, belgiumyp.com, localsmall.business, trendyflash.nl.

## Beyond links: two channels the research kept surfacing

**YouTube is your most under-exploited asset.** An Ahrefs study of 75,000 brands found
YouTube mentions correlated with AI-search visibility at r=0.737, versus 0.218 for backlinks.
You have set recordings and event footage. A channel costs nothing but time, and every video
is a second search surface. Note the GDPR constraint: get consent before publishing footage
of identifiable guests.

**Reviews are a ranking factor, not just social proof.** Controlled tests show crossing
**10 reviews** produces a measurable lift. Ask everyone — never only the happy ones, which is
review gating and gets *all* your reviews removed. Roughly five per month reads as organic;
requesting forty at once from your back catalogue is the single worst move on a new profile.

## Realistic timeline

- **Weeks 1–4:** Tier 1 + Tier 2. Free, and the Greek cluster is uncontested.
- **Months 2–3:** Tier 3 and 4. Venue links as you play each room.
- **Months 4–9:** first meaningful organic movement. The Greek queries land first.

One honest expectation: a brand-new domain does not rank quickly no matter what you do. The
Business Profile and the Greek institutional cluster will bring bookings months before the
website rankings do.
