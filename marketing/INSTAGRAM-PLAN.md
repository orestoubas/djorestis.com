# @djorestis — Instagram & TikTok Launch Plan

Goal: a brand-first presence (DJORESTIS identity, not personal-profile content) that
generates bookings — corporate first, then weddings/baptisms, then private events —
with a sustainable **one post per week**.

## 1. Account setup (do once, ~30 minutes)

- [ ] Register **@djorestis** on Instagram AND TikTok (grab both even if TikTok stays quiet at first). If taken, fallbacks in order: `@djorestis.official`, `@djorestisbxl`, `@dj.orestis`.
- [ ] Instagram: switch to a **Business account** (category: *DJ* or *Entertainment service*) — unlocks insights, contact button and ads later.
- [ ] Profile photo: `marketing/social/avatar-1024.png` (the gold vinyl monogram — recognisable at thumbnail size).
- [ ] Bio (≤150 chars):
  > DJ · Brussels 🇧🇪
  > Corporate events · Weddings · Greek nights
  > Greek / Afro / Latin / RnB / Electronic
  > 📩 info@djorestis.com
- [ ] Link: `https://djorestis.com` (later swap to a link-in-bio only if genuinely needed — the site is the funnel).
- [ ] Contact buttons: email `info@djorestis.com`; add WhatsApp once the number is live.
- [ ] Story **Highlights** (create as content arrives): `Events` · `Weddings` · `Corporate` · `Greek Nights` · `Mykonos`.
- [ ] Follow & engage from day one: Brussels venues you play, Greek community orgs, wedding planners BE/NL, corporate event agencies, Brussels food/lifestyle pages.

## 2. The content system — balanced 4-week rotation

One post per week, cycling through four formats. Every post uses the brand look
(templates in `marketing/social/`, dark + champagne gold, Marcellus headlines).

| Week | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Event recap** (Reel ≤30s or 3-5 photo carousel) | Social proof — you exist, rooms are full |
| 2 | **DJ tip / behind the scenes** (talking text-card or short clip) | Authority + save-able content for couples & planners |
| 3 | **Music moment** (15-30s mix clip, track pairing, transition) | Credibility with music lovers; TikTok's favourite format |
| 4 | **Booking prompt** (branded card: availability, season reminder) | Direct response — the ask |

Rules of thumb: Reels > static for reach; captions in English with a Greek or French
line when the content suits it; always a location tag (Brussels venues rank in local
discovery); post consistently on the same weekday evening (Thu or Fri 18:00–20:00).

## 6. TikTok cross-posting rules

- Repost every **Reel** (weeks 1/3-format posts especially) to TikTok — export without the Instagram watermark (save the original file, don't re-download from IG).
- TikTok captions: shorter, one hook line + 3-4 hashtags (#greekdj #brussels #djlife + one topical).
- Music-moment clips are TikTok's native language — if one format overperforms there, double it.
- No TikTok-only content until Instagram runs effortlessly.

## 7. Monthly KPI check (5 minutes, first Monday)

- Followers (direction, not the number itself)
- Reach per post — which of the 4 formats wins? Do more of it
- Profile visits → website taps (Instagram Insights)
- **DMs/emails mentioning Instagram** — the only KPI that pays: log every enquiry source
- One action per review: drop the weakest post type, copy the strongest
