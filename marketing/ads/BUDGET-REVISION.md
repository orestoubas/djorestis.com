# Ads plan revised for a sub-€150/month budget

You told me your total monthly budget — ads, listings and tools combined — is under €150.
My earlier campaign assumed €10/day (€300/month). **That plan is not viable and you should
not run it.** Here is the honest redesign.

## The problem with thin ad budgets

At €2–5 per click for wedding keywords, €100/month buys roughly 20–50 clicks. At a realistic
3–6% enquiry rate that is **one to three enquiries a month, on a good month**. Meanwhile
those clicks land on a site with no photos and no reviews, so they convert at the low end
of that range.

Spreading a thin budget across three ad groups is the classic way to waste it: no group
gets enough data to optimise, and you learn nothing.

## What I recommend instead

**Do not run Google Ads yet.** Spend nothing on ads until two things are true:

1. You have real photographs on the site
2. You have roughly 10 Google reviews

Both are free. Both roughly double what the same €100 buys you. Starting ads before them
means paying to send people to a page that cannot close.

In the meantime, put the entire budget to work where it compounds:

| Item | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Photographer for one Papillon night | €150–300 **once** | Unblocks the website, the profile, social, and eventually the ads |
| Everything in `BACKLINKS-ACTION-PLAN.md` Tier 1 & 2 | **€0** | eventplanner.be, Infobel, Goudengids, WEWED, Cueup, the Greek institutions |
| Venue and planner outreach | **€0** | See `VENUE-OUTREACH-KIT.md`. The highest-value channel for a DJ |
| Email capture + nurture | **€0** on Brevo's free tier | Weddings decide over 6–18 months; without this you lose everyone not ready today |
| Review drip | **€0** | The single biggest ranking gap you have |

That is a genuinely competitive programme for zero recurring spend, and it fits 3–5 hours
a week.

## When you do start ads — the lean campaign

Once photos and ~10 reviews exist, start with **one ad group only**, on your cheapest and
highest-converting niche.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Search only. Untick Display Network |
| Daily budget | **€3/day** (≈€90/month) |
| Bidding | Maximise clicks, max CPC **€1.50** |
| Locations | Brussels + 25 km, **Presence: people in this location** |
| Ad group | **Greek nights only** |

Use the Greek ad group exactly as written in `GOOGLE-ADS-CAMPAIGN.md`, but restrict keywords
to exact match only:

```
[greek dj brussels]
[dj grec bruxelles]
[griekse dj brussel]
[greek dj belgium]
[greek party dj brussels]
[ελληνας dj βρυξελλες]
```

Why Greek first: almost no competition, so clicks are cheap; the intent is unmistakable;
and you are objectively the best answer, so the conversion rate is far above what you would
get bidding against an established competitor on "wedding DJ Brussels".

Add the full negative keyword list before launching. That list matters more at €3/day than
at €10/day — one wasted click is a third of your daily budget.

**Scale rule:** only add the wedding ad group once the Greek group has produced at least
three enquiries and you know your cost per enquiry. Never add a second ad group to fix a
first one that is not working.

## What to skip entirely at this budget

- **Meta / Instagram ads** — needs photos and video first, and a budget that can sustain
  testing. Revisit when the budget passes €300/month.
- **Retargeting** — worth doing eventually, but pointless before you have enough traffic to
  retarget.
- **Paid wedding directories** — House of Weddings and Mariages.net premium are €1,800+/year.
  Take the free listings only.
- **SEO tools** — Search Console and Analytics are free and give you what you need.

## The realistic expectation

At under €150/month you are not going to outspend anyone. You will win by being the obvious
answer in a niche nobody else serves, by being recommended by venues, and by having reviews
your competitors' newer rivals don't. That is slower than buying traffic, and it is more
durable.
