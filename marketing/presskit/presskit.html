DJ & Event Producer — Brussels

DJ Orestis is a Brussels-based DJ and event producer who moves fluently between Greek, electronic, Afro, Latin and RnB — a range built over nine years behind the decks, from three seasons in Mykonos nightlife to six years at the heart of the Brussels scene. Now in his fourth year as resident DJ at Papillon Schuman and twice host DJ of the Brussels Greek Food Festival, he delivers complete event productions — sound, light, photography and video — for celebrations of up to 500 guests, across six countries and in four languages.
