# Google Business Profile — setup for DJ Orestis

The single biggest lever for local searches ("wedding DJ Brussels", "DJ near me"),
and the only place Google reviews can live. Set up at business.google.com.

## Core settings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Business name | **DJ Orestis** |
| Primary category | **DJ** |
| Secondary categories | Event planner · Wedding service · Entertainer |
| Website | https://djorestis.com |
| Email | info@djorestis.com |
| Storefront location | **No** — customers do not visit you |
| Address | Your real address, entered for verification, kept **hidden** from the public profile |

Never use a fake address or a virtual office — Google suspends profiles for this and
reinstatement is slow and painful.

## Service areas (up to 20)

Brussels first, then the Belgian cities you actually work in:

Brussels · Leuven · Antwerp · Ghent · Liège · Namur · Charleroi · Bruges · Mechelen ·
Waterloo · Wavre · Hasselt — plus regions such as Flemish Brabant and Walloon Brabant.

Deliberately **not** listed: the Netherlands, France, Germany, the UK, Greece. Google
weights local proximity heavily; spreading the service area across six countries dilutes
Brussels relevance, which is where the bookings are. The website carries the
international message instead.

>
> Full-package event production is also available: professional sound and lighting, photography and video for events of up to 500 guests.
>
> Languages: Greek, English, French and Dutch.

Rules the text respects: keywords in the first sentence, no links, no prices, no
promotional offers, no superlative claims — all of which Google rejects. Client names
(Freshfields, BCG) are deliberately generalised to "leading international firms":
naming clients publicly without their sign-off is not worth the risk.

## Getting reviews (the part that actually drives bookings)

1. In your profile dashboard, copy the **review link** ("Ask for reviews" / "Get more reviews").
2. Send it after every event, while the client is still glowing — the next day, not next month.
3. Message template:

   > Hi [name], it was a pleasure playing at your [wedding / company party] on [date] —
   > thank you for having me. If you enjoyed the night, a short Google review would mean
   > a lot and helps other people find me: [review link]

4. Start with past clients you're still in touch with: Papillon Schuman, community
   organisers, recent couples. Ten genuine reviews put you ahead of most local competitors.
5. Reply to every review, positive or negative. Google rewards active profiles, and
   prospective clients read the replies.

Never pay for reviews or post them yourself — Google detects patterns and removes them,
and the profile can be penalised.

## Keep it alive

- Add photos as they arrive (see `PHOTOS.md`) — profiles with photos get markedly more clicks.
- Post updates for upcoming events (Google Posts) — free, and appear in your listing.
- Keep the name, address and phone identical to the website and invoices. Google cross-checks.
