Return to Mykonos

A guest set on the island where I learned this craft — three seasons of training, one unforgettable summer night back behind the decks.

Every DJ has a place that made them. Mine is Mykonos. Before Brussels, before the weddings and the corporate ballrooms, I spent three full seasons on that island — learning the trade the hard way, night after night, in front of the most demanding party crowds in Europe. So when a call came this summer inviting me back for a guest set, I did not check my calendar. I just said yes.

The island that trains you

People imagine Mykonos DJing as glamour. It is actually a boot camp. In a single season you play for every crowd on earth: American honeymooners at sunset, Italian groups at midnight, Athenians who know every lyric, London city workers on a 48-hour blowout — sometimes all in the same room, in the same hour. Three seasons of that teaches you things no course can: how to read fifty nationalities at a glance, how to hold a floor at 4 a.m., how to lift a sunset crowd without breaking its spell. It is the foundation of everything I now do as a DJ in Brussels, and of the Mykonos-style sets clients book me for across Europe.

The night itself

Stepping back into a booth on the island felt like coming home and sitting an exam at the same time. The Mykonos crowd owes you nothing — reputation means little after the first track; you earn the floor in real time or you lose it. I opened at golden hour with melodic, sun-soaked house as the sky did its nightly performance over the Aegean, then built through Mediterranean and Greek-flavoured grooves into a peak-time set that had the terrace jumping well past midnight.

Brussels taught me elegance. Mykonos taught me survival. A DJ needs both.

Somewhere in the middle of the set, I dropped a Greek classic reworked over a house rhythm and watched a table of internationals dance to a language they did not speak. That, in one image, is what I try to export from this island to every event I play in the north.

What I brought back in my suitcase

  • Fresh sound. A summer's worth of new island edits and Mediterranean house now woven into my Brussels sets.
  • Sharpened instincts. Nothing recalibrates your crowd-reading like the island that first taught it to you.
  • A promise. To keep one foot on the island — because the day this job stops feeling like that night on the terrace is the day to stop.

Bringing the island north

Autumn means back to Belgium — back to the residency, the weddings, the corporate season. But clients booking a Mykonos-style DJ set for a terrace party, a summer corporate event or a wedding cocktail hour should know: it is not a playlist, it is a feeling, and I carry it with me from the source.

Want that island energy at your own event, in Belgium or anywhere in Europe? Get in touch — the sunset set travels well.
